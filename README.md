# The Oaks Are Turning

A field guide to one weekend — Julian, California, November 6–8, 2026.

Two nights at a farmhouse on two fenced acres for Paul, Morgan, Elwood (border
collie) and Ellie (golden retriever), timed to the week San Diego County's black
oaks turn. Julian is an apple town; this is deliberately not an apple trip.

**Live:** https://pekohlmeier.github.io/julian-field-guide

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, no dependencies, no framework.

## What it does

- Live countdown to check-in
- Twelve activity cards, filterable by dogs / effort / fall color, with picks saved to `localStorage`
- Restaurant and private-chef comparison, including the real cost for a party of two
- Weather figures and a tappable packing checklist that persists
- Drifting leaf canvas on `<canvas>`, three-state theme toggle, full `prefers-reduced-motion` support
- Two dog cards with Web Audio synthesis — no audio files

## Built with

Designed in [Claude Design](https://claude.ai/design), implemented as vanilla
HTML/CSS/JS. Type is Fraunces (with the `WONK` axis on), Archivo, and DM Mono.
The palette is drawn from a California black-oak woodland: goldenrod, russet,
and lichen sage on an oat-bone ground.
